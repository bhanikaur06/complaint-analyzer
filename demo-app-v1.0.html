How a complaint is processed

    
Every stage is visible in the output, so no score is unexplainable.

    
    
      
  1. Normalize — trim whitespace and correct common misspellings and chat shorthand such as "plz" and "urgnt".
    2. 
      
  2. Classify emotion with the model — Naive Bayes over unigram and bigram features returns a probability for each of the six emotions.
    3. 
      
  3. Score emotion with the rules — weighted lexicon matching with intensifier, diminisher and negation handling, used to break ties and to supply the evidence highlighting.
    4. 
      
  4. Combine — in hybrid mode the model decides unless it is unconfident, in which case the rule engine's dominant emotion wins.
    5. 
      
  5. Score urgency — weighted urgency keywords, escalation threats, de-escalating phrases, capitals and exclamation marks, plus a share of the emotional intensity.
    6. 
      
  6. Apply the safety override — a hazard phrase forces urgency to at least 78, so a calmly worded emergency can never land in the low queue.
    7. 
      
  7. Route and schedule — keyword matching assigns a department, and the priority band sets the response deadline.
    8. 
      
  8. Draft a reply — a template is selected by emotion and priority and filled with the detected category.

Known limitations

    
Worth stating openly — these define the next iteration.

    
    
      
  • The corpus is authored for this project, not sampled from live support tickets, so accuracy demonstrates the method rather than production performance.
    • 
      
  • A hundred examples across six classes is a small training set; the rarer classes have very few examples each.
    • 
      
  • Sarcasm still inverts the result. The system flags conflicting signals but cannot resolve them.
    • 
      
  • Hinglish coverage is partial — a handful of common terms, not real code-mixed language support.
    • 
      
  • Only one emotion is reported as dominant, though the full profile is shown.
    • 
      
  • Urgency weights are hand-tuned rather than learned, because urgency labels are sparser than emotion labels.
